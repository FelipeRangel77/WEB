Our Philosophy

Only what
matters.

Percent was born from a simple idea: you only need a fraction of what you own. We design essential garments — pieces that pair with everything, last for years, and feel like an extension of who you are.

Every fabric is sourced from mills in Japan, Italy, and France. Every pattern is refined over months, not weeks. We believe that when you reduce quantity, you can invest fully in quality.
